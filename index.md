# Rootkit: 1999 privacy policy

_Last updated: 8 November 2026_

Rootkit: 1999 is made by cyberjangle101. This policy explains what data the Android app uses and why.

## What the game itself collects

Nothing. Your progress, settings and scores, including your Daily Run results, are saved only on
your device. The Daily Run gives every player the same board each day, but scores are not uploaded
and there is no online leaderboard. The game has no accounts, no analytics and no servers of its own.
Uninstalling the app deletes your save.

## Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows one full-screen ad after every three finished games. Ads are provided by
Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, your
approximate location (from your IP address), and information about your device and how you interact
with ads. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are
shown, and you can change your choice at any time in **SETTINGS > AD PRIVACY**. This choice only
decides whether ads are personalised: if you decline, you still see ads, but they are not based on
your data. The only way to remove ads is the "Support the developer" purchase below.

Google's use of this data is covered by Google's own privacy policy:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Google > Ads).

## Purchases (Google Play)

"Support the developer" is a one-off purchase made through Google Play, which removes all ads. Google
handles the payment; the game never sees your payment details. The game only asks Google Play whether
your account owns the purchase, so it can keep ads switched off after a reinstall.

## Children

Rootkit: 1999 is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date. Questions: open an
issue at https://github.com/cyberjangle101/rootkit-99-privacy/issues.
