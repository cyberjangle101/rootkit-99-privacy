# Rootkit 99

It's 1999. An intruder is on your box, and it wants /root.

Rootkit 99 is a real-time defence game for Android, played inside a glowing CRT terminal. Kill the
intruder's rogue processes, suspend the services it hijacks, firewall the ports it owns, and trace
it hop by hop until you can BLOCK its origin.

- **Story: The Resistance.** 30 days of missions, each with its own twist.
- **Quickplay.** Endless waves at Easy, Medium or Hard, with the worldwide top 5 for each shown in
  the game (Google Play Games).
- **Daily Run.** One timed board a day, the same layout for every player.
- 13 achievements and cloud save through Google Play Games, so your progress follows you to a new
  phone. Plays offline; Google Play Games is optional.

Rootkit 99 is free on Google Play, with a short ad every two games and a one-off "Support the
developer" purchase that removes every ad.
